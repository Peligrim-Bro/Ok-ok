2. content-matrix: editorial idea backlog for both channels.
3. hook-generator: alternative hooks grounded in confirmed facts.
4. reels-scripting/post-formatter: prepare drafts.
5. QA: check citations, video contract, partner links and no invented claims.
6. Queue: draft -> reviewed -> approved -> published; publishing disabled until verified.
7. Analytics: only connected measured data, exclude own testing where identifiable.
