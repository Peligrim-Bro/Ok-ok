# OK-OK.click: mission and captions

Approved by the owner, 23 October 2026. Source: `brand-copy.mjs`.

RU: **Твой опыт — чья-то безопасная поездка**

EN: **Your experience. Someone else’s safer journey.**

Use the exact motto as the permanent signature. It expresses the project's
mission, not a guarantee that a business or trip is safe. Keep the owner's name
out of public copy without separate approval. Use first person only for the
owner's confirmed experiences; keep other material attributed to its sources.

## Contextual calls

| Topic | RU | EN |
|---|---|---|
| A real place or visit | Был здесь? Оцени! | Been here? Rate it! |
| A discovery or recommendation | Знаешь хорошее место? Поделись! | Found a good place? Share it! |
| A checked scam/report topic | Столкнулся с обманом? Предупреди! | Encountered a scam? Warn others! |

Do not use the warning CTA to imply wrongdoing by an unverified business. Keep
sources, qualifications, addresses and important conditions visible. A fictional
Oki comic normally uses the discovery CTA and keeps its fiction disclosure.

## Implemented routes and limits

The website shows the mission in the existing contact/notes block. The adjacent
author chat includes a Place/review topic. Pulse ratings currently stay on the
device; do not claim they are published or aggregated for other visitors. A
recommendation/report shared through the author contact is reviewed separately.

Future Telegram entries use `brandCTA` metadata (`rate`/`share`/`warn`) and
`channelText`. Original post bodies, IDs, media, times and delivery keys stay
unchanged. The native publisher checks the complete caption before delivery.
Do not manually append another motto, remove sources to make space, send extra
messages or repost already-delivered content.

This standard supplies captions for future video preparation. Existing video
files and published posts are not rewritten. Paused TikTok/YouTube/Metricool
publishing stays paused; this branding task does not enable those schedulers.
